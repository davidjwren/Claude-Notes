Claude Notes is your notebook inside Claude: one private page that works on your phone, tablet and computer, with Claude built in.

## Start here
- **Write**: click into **Quick note** at the top of the sidebar, or press **New note**. Notes save as you type, in markdown.
- **Organise**: give a note tags such as `Work/Projects`. Each tag becomes a folder under **Folders**; a note can sit in several.
- **Find**: search looks at titles, tags, keywords and the text of every note. **Graph view** shows how your notes link.
- **To-do**: the **To-do** row at the top of the sidebar holds your to-do lists. Use **New to-do list** on the start page, or ask Claude "add to my to-do list: …".

## Claude, in your notes
- Open a note and use **Chat** (speech bubble) to ask about it, **Auto-improve** (wand) for keywords and links, or the sparkle beside a paragraph to rewrite, shorten or expand it.
- From any Claude chat, the `/notes` skill saves, finds and edits notes for you: "save this as a note", "find my notes on…".

## Good to know
- Your notes are stored in this page's database on Anthropic's servers, and sent to Claude when you use a Claude feature. Keep passwords, account numbers and confidential client or work material out.
- Download a copy at any time: **Settings → Download all (JSON)**.

The **Help** section in the sidebar explains everything else, starting with [[Claude Notes basics]].
